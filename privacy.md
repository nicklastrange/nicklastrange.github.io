# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 20, 2026**

## Introduction

DoMates ("we", "our", "the app") is a shared chore management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Display name** — The name you enter when setting up the app. This is stored locally on your device and shared with members of your chore lists for identification purposes.
- **Chore list data** — Task names, due dates, completion status, and recurring schedules that you create within the app.

### Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not require account registration, email addresses, passwords, or any personal credentials.
- We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or browsing history.
- We do not sell or share any personal data with third parties beyond what is described below.

## Data Storage

DoMates uses a **local-first architecture**. All your data is stored on your device. Data is synchronized with our servers only when you share a list with others, and only for the purpose of enabling multi-device sync between list members.

Our servers store only temporary synchronization snapshots. No long-term user profiles or accounts are maintained server-side.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data for personalized advertising as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can learn more about how Google uses data at: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications for chore reminders. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any server communication.

## Children's Privacy

DoMates does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
