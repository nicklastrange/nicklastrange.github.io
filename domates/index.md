# Support

Having trouble with DoMates? We're here to help.

---

## Common Questions

**How do I create a chore list?**
Open the app, enter your name, and tap "Create a new list". Give your list a name and you're ready to go.

**How do I invite someone to my list?**
Tap the invite icon on your list, and a 24-hour invite code will be generated. Share this code with the person you want to invite. They can enter it in the app to join your list.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
You can create and manage tasks offline. An internet connection is only needed for generating invite codes, joining lists, and syncing across devices.

**Can I use DoMates on multiple devices?**
Your chore lists sync automatically across devices when connected to the internet.

**How do recurring tasks work?**
When creating or editing a chore, you can set it to repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or on custom days of the week.

**How do notifications work?**
DoMates sends reminders before a task is due, at the due time, and if a task becomes overdue. Notifications respect silent hours (midnight to 8 AM). You can deny notification permission without affecting core functionality.
